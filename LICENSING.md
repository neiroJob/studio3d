## What needs a commercial license

| Use | Commercial license required |

| Offering Studio3D (or a modified version) to other companies as a hosted, cloud or SaaS service | ❌ Yes |
| Selling Studio3D, or a product or service that includes, embeds or requires it | ❌ Yes |
| Paid installation, support or customization packages built on Studio3D for third parties | ❌ Yes |

Not sure? Ask before you start — it is free to ask.

## Change Date

Each released version of Studio3D automatically becomes available under the **Apache License 2.0** four years
after it was first published. Newer versions stay under the BSL until their own Change Date.

## Getting a commercial license

- Open an issue with the **"Commercial license"** template: <https://github.com/neiroJob/studio3d/issues/new/choose>
  (do not put confidential details in a public issue — leave a contact and we will continue privately), or

Studio3D uses open source components under their own licenses (Django, HTMX, Alpine.js, PostgreSQL, Redis, Caddy
and others). The BSL applies only to Studio3D's own code. See [NOTICE](NOTICE).
